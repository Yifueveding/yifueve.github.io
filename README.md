            <h1>About Me</h1>
            <p>I am a Shell Energy Scholar at MIT Energy Initiative and MIT Sloan School of Management. I have been fortunate to be supervised by <a href="https://mitsloan.mit.edu/faculty/directory/thomas-magnanti">Professor Thomas Magnanti</a> and work with research scientist <a href="https://energy.mit.edu/profile/pablo-duenas-martinez/">Dr. Pablo Duenas-Martinez</a>.</p>
            
            <p>My research interests focus on data-driven operations and planning for sustainable energy and transportation systems under the transformation of the AI Era, by employing advanced stochastic models and machine learning techniques. At MIT Energy Initiative, I have been working on the net-zero Indian power system planning, with the founding director of the MIT Tata Center for Technology and Design, <a href="https://energy.mit.edu/profile/robert-stoner/">Dr. Robert Stoner</a> and <a href="https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/dharik-mallapragada">Prof. Dharik Mallapragada</a> at NYU. Previously, I have also been working with <a href="https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/benjamin-hobbs/">Prof. Benjamin Hobbs</a> on U.S. power market modelling.</p>
            
            <p>I hold a PhD in Engineering Science from the University of Oxford, where I worked with <a href="https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/malcolm-mcculloch/">Prof. Malcolm McCulloch</a>, <a href="https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/thomas-morstyn/">Prof. Thomas Morstyn</a> and other brilliant mentors.</p>
